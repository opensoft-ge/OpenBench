# Repository instructions

- Describe OpenBench as a standalone product in code, comments, documentation, prompts and metadata. Do not name, link to, compare with, or record checkout paths or revisions of other codebases used during development.
- Keep product capabilities, limitations and validation results accurate. An independent grading solution is part of the product's evaluation design, not a development-source attribution.
- Never add `Co-Authored-By` trailers. Check full commit messages before committing and verify task-created or amended commits before pushing.

## AI product direction

Requirement added 2026-11-16: OpenBench must become fully AI enabled, agentic and conversational across its core journeys. The [AI capability and acceptance guide](docs/ai-capabilities.md) defines the required work and evidence. This direction does not change the implementation scope or historical validation results recorded above.
